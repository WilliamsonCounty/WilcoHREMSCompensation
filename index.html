WilCo Total Compensation Estimator

At Williamson County, our employees enjoy a total compensation package that goes beyond salary alone - competitive pay, a state-leading retirement plan, and comprehensive benefit options set us apart. This interactive estimator illustrates the annual total compensation an individual (and their family!) receives when they work for Williamson County. Supplemental information on each type of compensation can be found by clicking the information icons (ⓘ) next to the section titles.

To use this tool, type in your salary and hit ENTER, then select your benefit plan option and coverage type, and click the “Calculate” button to see your annual total compensation estimate.

Please contact Human Resources if you have any questions or need help using this compensation estimator.

Retirement

Retirement

7% is deposited into your account and earns 7% compound interest annually. The benefit WilCo provides is based on your final account balance and employer matching. Our current match rate is 250%! One of the highest in the state of Texas. Once County employees become vested at 8 years of service (either directly with Williamson County or through combined service time from other qualified Texas retirement systems), you have a right to a lifetime monthly benefit that will include employer matching when you reach retirement eligibility and choose to retire. There are several ways to meet TCDRS retirement eligibility requirements. To find out more about the County's retirement program and/or eligibility requirements, please visit TCDRS.

IMPORTANT! Retirement contributions only apply once an employee is vested (completes 8 years of service).
